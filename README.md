# Super Car Garage

Browse hundreds of sports cars, from 1950s classics to today's hypercars, and build your own dream garage.

**Open the app:** use the GitHub Pages link in the "About" box on the right of this page.

## Features
- Photo grid of 600+ sports cars
- Filter by brand, search by name, sort A–Z, by brand or by age
- Car pages with the story from Wikipedia
- My Garage: star your favourite cars (saved in your browser)

## Add it to your iPhone home screen
1. Open the app link in **Safari**
2. Tap the **Share** button
3. Tap **Add to Home Screen**
