Super Car Garage

Privacy policy and support · Last updated 14 November 2026

Privacy policy

Super Car Garage doesn't collect, store or share any personal data. There are no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking.

What stays on your device

The cars you add to My Garage, and a copy of the car list so the app opens faster, are saved only on your iPhone or iPad. They are never sent to us. Deleting the app deletes them.

Services the app connects to

To show cars, the app downloads information and photos from these Wikimedia Foundation services:

  • Wikidata (car list and facts)
  • Wikipedia (car stories)
  • Wikimedia Commons (photos)

Like any website, these services receive ordinary request details such as your IP address. The app sends them nothing else about you. Their handling of that information is covered by the Wikimedia Foundation Privacy Policy.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages and collects no information from anyone, including children.

Your rights

Because we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete. If you have questions about privacy, including under UK GDPR, contact us using the details below.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date at the top.
